# Cepheus product

Cepheus publishes essays about technology, public institutions and political judgment. Its watercolor homepage remains an introduction to connected knowledge; it is not an institutional dataset.

“What We Owe to Each Other” now carries a bounded institutional research object beside its prose. Readers can follow six narrative states, explore one documented mechanism at a time, and inspect a finding's evidence. Publication separates observation, qualitative coding and reviewed analysis. The candidate must not be described as globally complete or as a numerical measure of power.

The research release is 1.0.0-rc.1 pending the author's editorial sign-off. Agent substantive review is disclosed. All languages retain public language selection; translated essay drafts remain explicitly drafts. The primary research records are English.

The old Gap Matrix, Friction, numerical frontiers and technical-capacity implementation are retired. Their useful research documentation is retained separately; no synthetic score or old graph runs in production or local preview.
